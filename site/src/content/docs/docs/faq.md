## The Settings and Watch later buttons don't show up

Both need a signed-in account, so Kempt adds them only when you're signed in.

## Something looks broken after a YouTube update

YouTube changes its page often and tests new layouts on some accounts. Turn the feature off in the popup to get YouTube's original back, then [open an issue](https://github.com/remonilo/kempt-yt/issues) with:

- your browser and its version,
- your window width,
- your YouTube language,
- signed in or out,
- the page and the steps that lead to the problem.

A screen recording helps with anything animated.

## Does it work with Return YouTube Dislike?

Yes. The dislike count shows inside Kempt's dislike button, and the ratio bar sits under the like and dislike pill.

## Does it work with other YouTube extensions?

Most extensions that add buttons or panels keep working. Anything that adds a box to the right column of the watch page lands in the Videos tab. If two extensions restyle the same thing, turn one of the two features off.

## Why is the Shorts chip still in search results?

Shorts shelves are hidden everywhere. The search filter chip has no marker that works in every language, so it stays.

## Why does the sidebar dot have no count?

YouTube's sidebar data only flags that a channel has new content, with no count. Counting would mean fetching every channel's uploads.

## Is there a Safari version?

No. Kempt supports Firefox 128+ and Chromium browsers.

## Why is the popup only in English?

The labels Kempt draws on YouTube follow YouTube's language. The popup isn't translated yet.
